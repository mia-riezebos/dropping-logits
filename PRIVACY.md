# Privacy policy

Dropping Logits collects no data.

- **Page text stays on your device.** The extension reads the text of the pages you visit only to replace AI marketing phrases, and it does that inside your browser. It never stores, sends, or shares that text.
- **One setting, stored locally.** It saves whether replacement is switched on, using the browser's extension storage, so the toolbar toggle applies to every tab. Nothing else is saved.
- **No network requests.** The extension contacts no server, loads no remote code, and includes no analytics or tracking.
- **No accounts.** There is nothing to sign up for and no personal information is asked for.

Because no data is collected, none is sold, transferred, or used for any other purpose.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/mia-riezebos/dropping-logits/issues.
